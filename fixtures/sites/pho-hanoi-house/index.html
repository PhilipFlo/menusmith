About Us

Pho Hanoi House began as a weekend pop-up between friends and grew into the neighborhood spot it is today. Everything is cooked from scratch, every day, with produce from farms we know by name.

Our menu follows the seasons, but the dishes our regulars love stay put. Ask your server about today’s specials and pairings — we are always happy to make a recommendation.

We seat about 70 guests inside and 24 on the patio, weather permitting. Reservations are recommended on weekends; walk-ins are always welcome at the bar.

Private Events

Planning a celebration? Our private dining room seats up to 40 guests and our events team will put together a custom menu for your occasion. Send us a note and we will get back to you within two business days.
