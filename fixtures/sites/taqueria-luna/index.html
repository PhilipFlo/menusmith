Our Story

Taqueria Luna began as a weekend pop-up between friends and grew into the neighborhood spot it is today. Everything is cooked from scratch, every day, with produce from farms we know by name.

Our menu follows the seasons, but the dishes our regulars love stay put. Ask your server about today’s specials and pairings — we are always happy to make a recommendation.

We seat about 70 guests inside and 24 on the patio, weather permitting. Reservations are recommended on weekends; walk-ins are always welcome at the bar.
